Good to know

Questions we hear first.

When should I involve an architect?

Before you finalise a plot, a lease or a layout. Early decisions are cheapest to change.

Do you also build the project?

No. SK Atelier provides architectural and interior design and construction drawings. Construction is done by your contractor.

I only have a plot or a rough idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Share your location, plot size and what you want the space to do. We take it from there.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Our projects span Bengaluru, Chitradurga, Mysuru and nearby towns.
